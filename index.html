About Nirdesh

Nirdesh Silwal is a Political Scientist, Academic, and dedicated leader of the Rastriya Swatantra Party. He has been actively engaged in alternative politics since 2012 AD. A Gold Medalist in Political Science and International Relations who also studies and teaches law, he brings both academic excellence and legal expertise. With a clear vision for Lamjung's development, he stands as a candidate who listens and acts.
